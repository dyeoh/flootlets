---
title: Accessibility
description: What flootlets does for accessibility, and what's still up to you.
---

Every component is built and tested against the same checklist.

## What flootlets handles

- **Keyboard:** everything works without a mouse, and keyboard focus is always visible.
- **Names:** controls have accessible names; hidden labels (like "Original price" or "opens in
  a new tab") are included for screen readers.
- **Buttons don't submit forms by accident:** `Button` is `type="button"` unless you say
  otherwise.
- **Loading:** a loading button stays focused, says it's busy (`aria-busy`) and ignores clicks.
- **Contrast:** every text and background token pair meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in light and dark,
  enforced by a test.
- **Motion:** animations stop or soften when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Pointer cursor** on everything clickable, including Kobalte's headless widgets.
- **Server rendering:** pages work and read correctly before JavaScript loads.

## What's up to you

- **Write meaningful text:** button labels that say what happens ("Add to cart", not "Click
  here"), and alt text that describes product photos.
- **Icon-only buttons** need a label: wrap one in [`VisuallyHidden`](/flootlets/components/visually-hidden/).
- **Keep your overrides readable:** if you change colour tokens, keep text at 4.5:1 contrast.
- **Use the same locale** for prices on the server and in the browser.
